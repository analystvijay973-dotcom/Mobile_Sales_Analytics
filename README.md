# Mobile_Sales_Analytics
Built an interactive Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard using Power BI with KPI cards, slicers, and dynamic visualizations.
Analyzed total sales, quantity, transactions, and customer ratings across multiple cities and mobile brands.
Developed monthly sales and quantity trend reports to monitor business performance and seasonal demand.
Created visualizations for payment methods, mobile models, and brand-wise sales analysis.
Implemented map-based reporting for city-wise sales tracking and geographical business insights.
Used DAX measures and Power Query for data cleaning, transformation, and dashboard optimization.
Generated actionable insights through interactive charts, trend analysis, and business reporting.
Designed customer rating and transaction analysis reports to support business decision-making.
